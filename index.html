Machined here.

Magellan machines parts for the F404 and F414, the afterburning engines in the Super Hornet, the Growler and the Gripen. In April 2026, Ottawa put $8M into its new Winnipeg machining centre.

Overhauled here.

StandardAero overhauls engines in the city where the company started. Its Winnipeg expansion opened on September 3, 2026, with about 40 percent more space for CF34 and CFM56 engines.

Tested here.

In Thompson, GLACIER runs engines of up to 150,000 lb of thrust through natural icing, year round. Rolls-Royce and Pratt & Whitney operate it with NRC. In Winnipeg, GE runs an engine research and test centre.

Manitoba is organising for defence.

Government, industry, colleges, universities and the Canadian Armed Forces now work as one alliance, with a dedicated defence lead based with us. Here is 2026 so far.

  1. April 24, 2026 $19.5M in federal defence investment for three Winnipeg projects, including Magellan and StandardAero. More than 150 jobs created.
  2. September 8, 2026 The Manitoba-Canada Defence Alliance launches, with a defence lead based at Manitoba Aerospace & Defence.
  3. September 16, 2026 Over $3.8M for the Defence and Dual-Use Innovation Bridge, to bring 100 Manitoba SMEs into defence supply chains.
  4. September 28, 2026 UK delegates tour Boeing, StandardAero and Magellan at the first Manitoba-UK Defence Summit.

One alliance, and one defence lead to call.

More than engines.

Composites, rockets and the maintenance behind every flight hour.

Composites

Boeing Winnipeg is Canada's largest aerospace composite plant, building here since 1971.

Rockets and satellites

Magellan builds Black Brant sounding rockets and satellite buses in Winnipeg.

Maintenance and support

KF Aerospace has supported DND programs for more than 25 years. PAL Aerospace adds ISR, in-service support and aircrew training.

Who builds it.

Members make, fix, test and teach. Tell us what you need built and we will make the introduction.
